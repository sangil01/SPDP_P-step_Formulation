## Motivation

The original treatment transition emptied every onboard full skip assigned to a visited treatment location. When two full skips shared the same treatment location, this excluded the choice of emptying one skip now and the other at a later visit. The revised transition keeps the same state representation but, when two eligible full skips are onboard, generates the three outcomes that empty either one or both. If only one eligible full skip is onboard, it is emptied.

This change enlarges only the edge set: the vehicle-state set is unchanged, while additional parallel edges represent the partial-emptying outcomes.
