## P-step definition
### Definition

A pattern  (= P-step) is defined as

$$
r = (P_r, \tau_r).
$$

- $P_r = (e_1, \ldots, e_q) = (v_0, \ldots, v_q)$
- If the first edge does not start at depot $0$, then $q = p$
- Otherwise, $q \le p$
- $\tau_r$: time immediately after servicing the first node of the p-step

### Feasibility

Let

$$
t(r) = \sum_{e \in P_r} t_e.
$$

Then a feasible p-step must satisfy

$$
0 \le \tau_r + t(r) \le T,
$$

and edge/state continuity:

$$
v_{e_i} = u_{e_{i+1}} \qquad \forall i = 1, \ldots, q-1,
$$

$$
\sigma(v_{e_i}) = \sigma(u_{e_{i+1}}) \qquad \forall i = 1, \ldots, q-1.
$$

### Concatenation

For a front p-step $r$ and a rear p-step $s$:

$$
v_q(r) = v_0(s),
$$

$$
\sigma(v_q(r)) = \sigma(v_0(s)),
$$

$$
\tau_r + t(r) = \tau_s.
$$

## Master problem

### Sets

$V = \{0,1,\ldots,2n+1\},$ customer node set.
$E=$ all feasible arc set.
$R^p =$ set of all feasible p-steps.
$\Sigma^+_{i}\ (\forall i\in V\setminus \{0,2n+1\})=$ set of all feasible state after serving $i$.
### Parameters


The cost of a p-step is

$$
c_r = \sum_{e \in P_r} c_e.
$$

The arc-incidence coefficient is

$$
b_e^r =
\begin{cases}
1 & \text{if } e \in P_r,\\
0 & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
$$

The visit coefficient is

$$
a_r^i =
\begin{cases}
2 & \text{if } i \text{ is an interior point of } P_r,\\
1 & \text{if } i \text{ is a start/end point of } P_r,\\
0 & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
$$

For time-state balance, define

$$
(s_r^{i,\sigma}, q_r^{i,\sigma}) =
\begin{cases}
(1, \tau_r) & \text{if } i = v_0(r) \text{ and } \sigma(v_0(r))=\sigma,\\
(-1, -(\tau_r + t(r))) & \text{if } i = v_p(r) \text{ and } \sigma(v_p(r))=\sigma,\\
(0,0) & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
$$

### Decision variables

$$
x_r \ge 0 \qquad \forall r \in R^p
$$

$$
\theta_e \in \{0,1\} \qquad \forall e \in E
$$

### Formulation

The master problem is

$$
\min \sum_{r \in R^p} c_r x_r
$$

subject to

$$
\sum_{r \in R^p} a_r^i x_r = 2
\qquad \forall i \in V \setminus \{0, 2n+1\}
$$

$$
\sum_{r \in R^p} s_r^{i,\sigma} x_r = 0
\qquad \forall i \in V \setminus \{0, 2n+1\},\ \forall \sigma \in \Sigma_i^+
$$

$$
\sum_{r \in R^p} q_r^{i,\sigma} x_r \ge 0
\qquad \forall i \in V \setminus \{0, 2n+1\},\ \forall \sigma \in \Sigma_i^+
$$

$$
\sum_{r \in R^p} b_e^r x_r = \theta_e
\qquad \forall e \in E
$$

$$
x_r \ge 0
\qquad \forall r \in R^p
$$

$$
\theta_e \in \{0,1\}
\qquad \forall e \in E
$$

## Compact P-step set

The compact p-step set $R^p$ includes:

1. all p-steps $r$ that start at $0$ with prior time $\tau_r = 0$,
2. all p-steps $r$ that do not start at $0$ and do not end at $2n+1$ with prior time

$$
\tau_r \in \{0,\, T - t(r)\},
$$

3. all p-steps $r$ that do not start at $0$ and end at $2n+1$ with prior time

$$
\tau_r = T - t(r).
$$

## Eq. (35): lower bound on the number of vehicles entering the subset of nodes

Six lower-bound families are:

$$
\sum_{e \in E} P_e^{\mathrm{in}} \theta_e \ge \left\lceil \frac{n}{2} \right\rceil, \qquad
\sum_{e \in E} P_e^{\mathrm{out}} \theta_e \ge \left\lceil \frac{n}{2} 
\right\rceil, 
$$

$$

\sum_{e \in E} E_e^{\mathrm{in}} \theta_e \ge \left\lceil \frac{n}{2} \right\rceil,
\qquad
\sum_{e \in E} E_e^{\mathrm{out}} \theta_e \ge \left\lceil \frac{n}{2} \right\rceil,
$$

$$
\sum_{e \in E} D_e^{\mathrm{in}} \theta_e \ge \left\lceil \frac{n}{2} \right\rceil, \qquad
\sum_{e \in E} D_e^{\mathrm{out}} \theta_e \ge \left\lceil \frac{n}{2} \right\rceil.
$$

The indicator definitions are:

- $P_e^{\mathrm{in}} = 1$ if $v_e$ is pick-up type, $u_e$ is $0$ or delivery type, or $I_{\pi_e}=1$
- $P_e^{\mathrm{out}} = 1$ if $u_e$ is pick-up type, $v_e$ is $2n+1$ or delivery type, or $I_{\pi_e}=1$
- $D_e^{\mathrm{in}} = 1$ if $v_e$ is delivery type, $u_e$ is $0$ or pick-up type, or $I_{\pi_e}=1$
- $D_e^{\mathrm{out}} = 1$ if $u_e$ is delivery type, $v_e$ is $2n+1$ or pick-up type, or $I_{\pi_e}=1$
- $E_e^{\mathrm{in}} = 1$ if $I_{\pi_e}=1$
- $E_e^{\mathrm{out}} = 1$ if $I_{\pi_e}=1$
- $I_{\pi_e}=1$ if the landfill sequence $\pi_e$ is not empty

## Eq. (36): location-based lower bound

The original COR-style inequality uses the inbound direction. For the p-step
theta formulation, the same location-block idea can also be written for the
outbound direction.

Inbound lower-bound families are:

$$
\sum_{e \in E} P_{e,o}^{\mathrm{in}} \theta_e
\ge
\left\lceil \frac{|N_{P,o}|}{2} \right\rceil
\qquad \forall o \in O_P,
$$
$$
\sum_{e \in E} E_{e,o}^{\mathrm{in}} \theta_e
\ge
\left\lceil \frac{|N_{E,o}|}{2} \right\rceil
\qquad \forall o \in O_E,
$$
$$
\sum_{e \in E} D_{e,o}^{\mathrm{in}} \theta_e
\ge
\left\lceil \frac{|N_{D,o}|}{2} \right\rceil
\qquad \forall o \in O_D,
$$
where $N_{P,o}$, $N_{E,o}$, and $N_{D,o}$ are the pickup,
treatment, and delivery action nodes associated with location $o$, and
$O_P$, $O_E$, and $O_D$ are the corresponding nonempty location sets.

The indicator definitions are:

- $P_{e,o}^{\mathrm{in}} = 1$ if $v_e \in N_{P,o}$ and the compressed edge enters the pickup-location block $N_{P,o}$ from outside. Equivalently, $P_{e,o}^{\mathrm{in}} = 1$ if $v_e \in N_{P,o}$ and either $\pi_e \ne \emptyset$ or $u_e \notin N_{P,o}$.
- $D_{e,o}^{\mathrm{in}} = 1$ if $v_e \in N_{D,o}$ and the compressed edge enters the delivery-location block $N_{D,o}$ from outside. Equivalently, $D_{e,o}^{\mathrm{in}} = 1$ if $v_e \in N_{D,o}$ and either $\pi_e \ne \emptyset$ or $u_e \notin N_{D,o}$.
- $E_{e,o}^{\mathrm{in}}$ is the number of entries into treatment location $o$ inside the compressed treatment sequence $\pi_e$. In the current capacity-2 implementation, $\pi_e$ does not contain duplicate copies of the same treatment location, so this reduces to $E_{e,o}^{\mathrm{in}} = 1$ if $o \in \pi_e$ and $0$ otherwise.

The symmetric outbound version is:

$$
\sum_{e \in E} P_{e,o}^{\mathrm{out}} \theta_e
\ge
\left\lceil \frac{|N_{P,o}|}{2} \right\rceil
\qquad \forall o \in O_P,
$$
$$
\sum_{e \in E} E_{e,o}^{\mathrm{out}} \theta_e
\ge
\left\lceil \frac{|N_{E,o}|}{2} \right\rceil
\qquad \forall o \in O_E,
$$
$$
\sum_{e \in E} D_{e,o}^{\mathrm{out}} \theta_e
\ge
\left\lceil \frac{|N_{D,o}|}{2} \right\rceil
\qquad \forall o \in O_D.
$$

The outbound indicators are:

- $P_{e,o}^{\mathrm{out}} = 1$ if $u_e \in N_{P,o}$ and the compressed edge leaves the pickup-location block $N_{P,o}$. Equivalently, $P_{e,o}^{\mathrm{out}} = 1$ if $u_e \in N_{P,o}$ and either $\pi_e \ne \emptyset$ or $v_e \notin N_{P,o}$.
- $D_{e,o}^{\mathrm{out}} = 1$ if $u_e \in N_{D,o}$ and the compressed edge leaves the delivery-location block $N_{D,o}$. Equivalently, $D_{e,o}^{\mathrm{out}} = 1$ if $u_e \in N_{D,o}$ and either $\pi_e \ne \emptyset$ or $v_e \notin N_{D,o}$.
- $E_{e,o}^{\mathrm{out}}$ is the number of exits from treatment location $o$ inside the compressed treatment sequence $\pi_e$. In the current capacity-2 implementation, this is again equivalent to $E_{e,o}^{\mathrm{out}} = 1$ if $o \in \pi_e$ and $0$ otherwise.

A lighter combined version can replace the separate inbound and outbound rows:

$$
\sum_{e \in E}
\left(P_{e,o}^{\mathrm{in}} + P_{e,o}^{\mathrm{out}}\right)\theta_e
\ge
2\left\lceil \frac{|N_{P,o}|}{2} \right\rceil
\qquad \forall o \in O_P,
$$
$$
\sum_{e \in E}
\left(E_{e,o}^{\mathrm{in}} + E_{e,o}^{\mathrm{out}}\right)\theta_e
\ge
2\left\lceil \frac{|N_{E,o}|}{2} \right\rceil
\qquad \forall o \in O_E,
$$
$$
\sum_{e \in E}
\left(D_{e,o}^{\mathrm{in}} + D_{e,o}^{\mathrm{out}}\right)\theta_e
\ge
2\left\lceil \frac{|N_{D,o}|}{2} \right\rceil
\qquad \forall o \in O_D,
$$
with analogous combined rows for treatment and delivery. Separate inbound and
outbound rows imply the combined row, so the combined version is weaker but uses
fewer constraints.

## Eq. (37), Eq. (39), Eq. (40), Eq. (41), Eq. (43), and Eq. (44)

- Eq. (37) and Eq. (39) (lifted subtour elimination constraints): hard to incorporate since there are no  $N_T$
- Eq. (40) and Eq. (41): **when constructing the multi-graph**, **prune one direction if two arcs** connect the same physical location in opposite directions.

- Eq. (43): **within a p-step**, if multiple requests of the same type appear, enforce pick-ups in increasing `request_id` order.

- Eq. (44): enforce a minimum number of vehicles

$$
\sum_{e: u_e = 0} \theta_e \ge k_{\min}.
$$
