## Root node column generation

### Two-phase simplex method

The root-node CG procedure uses a two-phase primal simplex method.

### Phase 1

Generate initial columns to obtain a feasible solution at the root node.

The phase-1 formulation is

$$
\min \sum_i z_i + \sum_{i,\sigma} z_{i,\sigma}^+ + \sum_{i,\sigma} z_{i,\sigma}^- + \sum_{i,\sigma} h_{i,\sigma}
$$

subject to

$$
\sum_{r \in R^p} a_r^i x_r + z_i = 2
\qquad \forall i \in V \setminus \{0, 2n+1\}
$$

$$
\sum_{r \in R^p} s_r^{i,\sigma} x_r + z_{i,\sigma}^+ - z_{i,\sigma}^- = 0
\qquad \forall i \in V \setminus \{0, 2n+1\},\ \forall \sigma \in \Sigma_i^+
$$

$$
\sum_{r \in R^p} q_r^{i,\sigma} x_r + h_{i,\sigma} \ge 0
\qquad \forall i \in V \setminus \{0, 2n+1\},\ \forall \sigma \in \Sigma_i^+
$$

$$
\sum_{r \in R^p} b_e^r x_r = \theta_e
\qquad \forall e \in E
$$

$$
x_r \ge 0
\qquad \forall r \in R^p
$$

$$
1 \ge \theta_e \ge 0
\qquad \forall e \in E
$$
