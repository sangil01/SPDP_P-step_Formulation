
## Exact-refinement procedure

Let

$$
K(y)=
\sum_{\substack{e\in E:u_e=0\\v_e\in C^p}}y_e
$$

be the number of actual depot departures. The exact minimum number of vehicles is

$$
k_{\min}^{*}
=
\min\{K(y):y\text{ is a feasible integer solution of the SPDP}\}.
$$

The refinement starts from a valid lower bound

$$
L=
\max\left\{
k_{\min}^{\mathrm{COR}},
k_{\min}^{\mathrm{IP^B}},
k_{\min}^{\mathrm{VA}}
\right\},
$$

where only the enabled bounds are included. For each $m=L,L+1,\ldots$, it solves the two-index formulation on the multigraph after adding

$$
K(y)=m.
$$

The objective minimizes total duration only to guide the feasibility search, and `SolutionLimit = 1` terminates the solve as soon as an integer feasible solution is found. If the model with $K(y)=m$ is proven infeasible, then $k_{\min}^{*}\ge m+1$ and the next value is tested. If a feasible solution is found after all smaller candidates have been excluded, then

$$
k_{\min}^{*}=m.
$$

In particular, when the first candidate $m=L$ is feasible, validity of the initial lower bound gives $L\le k_{\min}^{*}$, while the feasible solution gives $k_{\min}^{*}\le L$; hence $k_{\min}^{*}=L$. Solver optimality is unnecessary for this certification.
